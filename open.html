Open stats

Live numbers from our site.

Why publish it? Well, we believe in transparency, which is baked into the tools we ship and why they're open source. Because we do gather stats, it makes sense that they should be public, so you can have all the facts to make a decision.

The dashboard below has visitors and page views, where they came from (search, direct, referring sites), which pages are popular, and a rough country breakdown. We chose simpleanalytcics as being one of the most privacy respecting tools, also run by a small team.

Can't see the dashboard above? Some browsers and content blockers will refuse to load it being an analytics tool. You try directly at dashboard.simpleanalytics.com/privacykey.org, or check our raw data exports in the website repo.

How this is collected

One small JavaScript file (~3KB) loaded on each page sends a single beacon when the page becomes visible. The beacon contains the page URL, the referrer (if any), the screen size bucket, and the page-load timing. No cookies are set. No localStorage is touched. No fingerprint is built. Simple Analytics resolves the request IP to a country and discards the IP itself.

If you have Do Not Track turned on in your browser, the beacon is skipped entirely. If you block scripts.simpleanalyticscdn.com at the DNS or extension layer, the beacon is skipped entirely. The site works fine without it.

For the full methodology, see docs.simpleanalytics.com/what-we-collect.
