Privacy on this site.

Last updated: 30 April 2026

privacykey is an organisation that builds privacy tools, so the rules we apply to this site are the rules we'd apply to any of them. Short version below; longer answers underneath. The numbers we collect aren't secret either — they're published live at privacykey.org/open.

The short version

  • Privacy-friendly analytics only. We use Simple Analytics — no cookies, no personal data. The numbers it produces are public on /open.
  • No accounts.
  • Static site. No server-side logic. The HTML you load is the HTML on disk.
  • Two third parties may receive your IP / User-Agent on a normal page load — our host (GitHub), and Simple Analytics. They're listed below.

What we collect

We use Simple Analytics to count page views and referrers in aggregate. It does this without setting cookies, without fingerprinting your browser, and without storing any data that could identify you personally. Their privacy posture and methodology are documented at docs.simpleanalytics.com/what-we-collect.

Beyond that, there is no privacykey.org server tracking visits, building profiles, or storing identifiers. No cookies are set by this site itself. The site is a static set of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files served by our host.

The numbers Simple Analytics produces aren't a secret — the dashboard is published in full at privacykey.org/open.

Third parties your browser may contact using our site

  • Simple Analytics (scripts.simpleanalyticscdn.com, queue.simpleanalyticscdn.com) — loads a small script (~3KB) and pings a beacon with the page URL and referrer. No cookies, no IDs, no fingerprints. They strip your IP after deriving the country and discard the rest. If JavaScript is disabled, only a fallback pixel fires (same data-shape, no script execution). You can opt out by enabling Do Not Track in your browser — Simple Analytics honours it.
  • GitHub — the static-hosting for our site. Receives standard HTTP request logs (IP, time, path) for delivery and abuse-prevention. We don't run our own analytics on those logs.

That's the lot. Fonts (Inter, JetBrains Mono, Noto Sans SC) and the Three.js library that powers the decorative rain animation are self-hosted — your browser only ever talks to our origin for them. Their licences are listed at legal.

If Simple Analytics bothers you, the site degrades gracefully — no rain disappears, no fonts swap out, the analytics ping just doesn't fire.

Going fully offline-ish

If you'd rather make zero third-party requests:

  • Block *.simpleanalyticscdn.com in your browser or DNS. The site will work normally — only the analytics ping is skipped.
  • Turn on Do Not Track in your browser — Simple Analytics will skip the ping by itself, no extension required.
  • Or read the underlying source on our GitHub — the same content as a flat repo.
