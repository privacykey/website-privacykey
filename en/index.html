Our values.

We strive to break down barriers, to make the technical more accessible and clear. Our tools help people understand, and take control of their personal data. These values guide what we do, why we create, and who we are. We are:

Transparent

Open about what we do, the tools we use, the libraries we ship.

Innovative

Be the change we want to see. Spark it with resourcefulness, sustain it with continuous improvement.

Actionable

Give people the power to make a difference — a toggle, a switch, an app, a deliverable.

Simplifying

Make the complex, clear.
